# Sponsor brand assets

Retrieved September 21, 2026 for the Agentic App event's sponsor section. Files are hosted locally and retain their original colors, proportions and bytes. Logo surfaces stay white in both page themes.

- `minimax-logo.png`: original navigation logo from the [MiniMax official website](https://www.minimaxi.com/), downloaded from the image URL referenced by its navigation.
- `kimi-logo.svg`: `Lockup_Main_Light.svg` from the logo archive offered by the [Kimi official brand guide](https://www.kimi.com/resources/kimi-brand). This is the lockup intended for light backgrounds.
- `provenance.json`: original download URLs, archive entry and SHA-256 hashes.

The brand marks belong to their respective owners. Kimi's brand guide links to its [logo usage terms](https://www.kimi.com/zh-cn/policies/logo-usage-terms).

Sponsor descriptions are based on the organizer-confirmed sponsorship: MiniMax provides RMB 50,000 worth of token credits across competition rewards and participant development; Kimi provides coding benefits without a public account quantity or promised duration. Product descriptions are supported by the MiniMax website and [Kimi Code](https://www.kimi.com/code/). These credits do not create an additional cash prize pool.
